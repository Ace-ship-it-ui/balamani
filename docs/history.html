Reason Behind Balamani Shih Tzu

Our Story

The inspiration behind Balamani Shih Tzu came from a deeply personal experience with our daughter while she was hospitalized in the UK.

During her stay in intensive care, she had regular visits from a therapy Shih Tzu. We noticed something remarkable—whenever the little dog was by her side, her heart rate would improve, and when the dog left, it would drop again. Seeing the positive impact that this gentle companion had on her recovery left a lasting impression on our family.

After that experience, we began searching for the right breed to inspire other children. We wanted a dog that was hypoallergenic, sturdy enough for a child, calm in nature, yet still playful and affectionate. After researching different breeds, we found that the Shih Tzu, an ancient breed originating from China, was the perfect match.

Shih Tzus are loyal, loving, and gentle companions. They enjoy cuddles, are happy to relax with their families, and are playful enough to enjoy rough-and-tumble games with children. Their affectionate temperament and adaptable nature made them the ideal choice for our family.

That life-changing experience inspired us to establish Balamani Shih Tzu, where our goal is to raise healthy, well-socialized puppies that bring the same love, comfort, and joy to other families that our Shih Tzus have brought to ours.

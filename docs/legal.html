Responsible Breeding and Animal Welfare

Balamani Shih Tzu is committed to responsible breeding and the health and welfare of its dogs and puppies. Prospective owners may be asked to provide information about their home, experience and ability to care for a Shih Tzu.

The breeder reserves the right to refuse or cancel a sale where there are reasonable concerns regarding the puppy's welfare or the prospective owner's suitability.

Buyer Registration Form

Prospective buyers who select a puppy will be asked to complete a form containing their personal details and information about the chosen puppy. This may include the buyer's name, address, contact details, identification details and the puppy's name, sex, colour, date of birth or microchip number, where available.

The information will be used to:

  • Identify the buyer and the selected puppy
  • Manage scheduled visits
  • Prepare the reservation or sales agreement
  • Maintain relevant business and animal records
  • Complete the transfer of ownership

Information will be handled in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation and applicable Maltese data-protection law.

Completing the form does not by itself confirm ownership or reservation of the puppy. A reservation is only confirmed when the breeder provides written confirmation and any agreed deposit has been received.

Weekly Visits

Once a puppy has been selected, buyers are expected to attend a scheduled visit each week until the puppy is ready to leave the breeder. These visits allow the buyer to become familiar with the puppy, observe its development and receive guidance regarding feeding, grooming, training and general care.

Visits must be arranged with the breeder in advance. Buyers must follow any hygiene, safety and animal-welfare instructions provided during the visit. The breeder may postpone or cancel a visit where necessary to protect the health and welfare of the puppies and adult dogs.

If a buyer cannot attend a scheduled visit, they must inform the breeder as early as possible. Repeated failure to attend without reasonable notice may affect the reservation, subject to the terms of the written reservation agreement.

Any rules concerning cancellation or the return of a deposit must be clearly stated in the written reservation agreement.
